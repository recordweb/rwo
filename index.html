RecordWeb Ontology (RWO)

Namespace: https://w3id.org/recordweb/ontology#

Version: 0.1.0 · Status: Draft · Turtle source

RWO defines the RDF/OWL vocabulary used for semantic interoperability of the RecordWeb Protocol (RWP) and the RecordWeb Concept (RWC). The central class rw:Record is a specialization of prov:Entity from the W3C PROV-O ontology.

Namespace prefix

@prefix rw:   <https://w3id.org/recordweb/ontology#> .
@prefix prov: <http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#> .
